What to expect

Same kind of look we give a neighbor who pulls in and says, “Can you just tell me what I am looking at?”

  • Tires. Wear, pressure, and what the tread is telling us.
  • Filters. A quick check of the ones that keep the car happy.
  • Fluids. Oil, coolant, washer fluid, and the rest of the usuals.
  • Dashboard lights. If something is on, we will talk through it in plain English.
  • A look-over of the car. The stuff you notice when you get around it and underneath.
  • Your name on the list. We keep a simple sheet so Saturday stays calm.

Saturday morning is the main window. If that is tight, look-overs can continue through October 3. Call the shop and we will set a time.
